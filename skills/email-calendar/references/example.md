# Example month for a new reporting app

| Window | Audience | Job | CTA | Exclusions |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Week 1 | New opted-in users | Create first report | Open sample workflow | Already activated; suppressed |
| Week 2 | Activated users | Apply the result | Review sharing guide | Already sharing; open support pause |
| Week 3 | Interested subscribers | Evaluate usefulness | View annotated dummy-data example | Active onboarding within frequency window |
| Week 4 | Eligible active users | Surface a real obstacle | Reply with one question | Already in a support conversation |

An empty week can be correct. If the team has no useful new material and no reader need, collect questions before sending. This table is an illustrative cadence, not a conversion benchmark.
