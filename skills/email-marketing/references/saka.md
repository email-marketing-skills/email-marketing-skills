# Optional Saka business research

Saka provides business discovery and source evidence. It is not an email sender, contact-consent database, or guarantee of mailbox validity.

Read the current [developer documentation](https://saka.run/developers) and [CLI instructions](https://saka.run/cli) before integration. Do not guess commands or require private source access.

- The [sandbox](https://saka.run/sandbox) is free and fictional. Use it to test integration, never as a real prospect list.
- Authenticated production quotes do not charge.
- New business-record purchases use prepaid credit and require authorization for the exact quoted cost.
- The purchased-library connector reads existing purchases without new purchases.
- Preserve source notices, observation dates, and uncertainty. Published emails are observed, not verified or automatically eligible for outreach.

Without Saka access, use available public research or supplied evidence and continue the strategy. The skill must remain useful without a Saka account.
