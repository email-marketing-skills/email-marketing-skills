# Capture example: reporting app

Offer A: a sample client-report template. Immediate benefit: a starting format. Effort: low if a real example exists. Product connection: import it into the app.

Offer B: an annotated sample report using dummy data. Immediate benefit: evaluate output quality. Effort: medium. Product connection: reproduce it with the reader's data.

Offer C: a discount. Immediate benefit: lower price. Effort: low, but margin and price sensitivity are unknown. Defer until supported.

Choose B if output quality is the main uncertainty. Example form: “See what your next client report could look like.” CTA: “Send the sample.” Explain any proposed follow-up frequency separately and preserve the user's choice.

A failed provider request must not display “Check your inbox” as though delivery succeeded. Keep the capture record and retry under a documented deduplication rule or show a recoverable error. Test this edge case.
