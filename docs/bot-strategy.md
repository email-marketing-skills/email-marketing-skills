# Email Marketing Desk launch strategy

## Audience and promise

Serve developers, solo founders and small software teams who have a product but no dedicated email marketer. Start with one useful customer action. The promise is a complete first campaign with a preview and a clear next decision; revenue and deliverability are not guaranteed.

## Product and distribution

The nine portable skills are the free foundation. Email Marketing Desk packages a repeatable operating loop for Grok Bot: brief → strategy → campaign → preview → review → next experiment. Keep first-run inputs to product/audience, activation goal, and existing list/provider. Existing signups favor activation; no list favors a useful signup offer. Account research remains a separate, optional lane.

Distribute the source package and reproducible setup through GitHub, and a dedicated page at saka.run/email-marketing-bot. Publish a public Grok template after native acceptance. Submit it to the Marketing category using the publisher's supported route. A template publication is not a curated listing. Until acceptance, the site offers a setup prompt and source link, never a fabricated import button or an endorsement claim.

## Conversion and economics

Free skills and draft workflows should be useful without purchasing anything. Agent/model fees and email provider costs remain separate. Offer Saka only when the user needs business discovery: inspect coverage, use a sample, obtain an explicit priced purchase decision, and measure qualified account usefulness. Do not turn bought business data into newsletter subscribers. Preserve existing Saka pricing and affiliate terms.

The funnel is marketplace or search → product brief → first usable campaign/preview → weekly review. Optional business research → Saka account → approved data purchase is a separate path. Measure page visits only through existing consented analytics; no new telemetry in the skills. Bot activation and repeat use require opt-in feedback or platform-provided metrics. Do not invent visibility into installed bots.

## Launch content

Use a fictional worked example with clearly labeled synthetic recipients. Show a welcome email, an activation exit, an opt-out skip and a weekly review that refuses a premature winner. Publish setup and capability limits alongside it. Initial discovery comes from the existing developer playbook, repository README and the requested marketplace submission. Social posts, outreach, paid promotion and affiliate messages require their own authorization.

## Acceptance and operations

Validate all packages and tests, run the behavioral cases, obtain independent review, verify native setup and fresh template import, then publish the actual import URL. Keep routines paused until the user's cadence/timezone and source are known. Treat per-user files as private; templates contain generic instructions only. Pin upgrades and evaluate before rollout. Record deployment, public template publication, submission and category acceptance separately.

Success measures: completed first campaign and preview; useful repeat review; reported blockers; optional research-to-purchase conversion where observed. Count actual events with defined denominators, not downloads as successful campaigns. Review early adopter feedback before adding a hosted sender, autonomous campaign execution or paid bot tier.
